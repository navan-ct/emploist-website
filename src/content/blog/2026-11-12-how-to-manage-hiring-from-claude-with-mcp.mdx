You can connect Claude to Emploist and manage your hiring by chatting. Ask which applicants fit a job best, move people to the next stage, leave notes for your team, or draft a new job, and Claude does it in Emploist for you.

This works through **MCP** (Model Context Protocol), an open standard that lets AI assistants connect to other apps. You don't need to know how it works to use it. Setting it up takes a few minutes and doesn't involve any code.

This guide shows how to connect Claude, what you can ask it to do, and how you stay in control. We'll use Fieldnote Labs, a sample company, for every example.

## What you need

- **An Emploist account.** Admins and members can both connect Claude.
- **Claude**, on the web at [claude.ai](https://claude.ai) or in the Claude desktop app.

On Claude's Team and Enterprise plans, an owner first adds Emploist in **Organization settings** → **Connectors**. You then click **Connect** next to it under **Customize** → **Connectors** and skip to [step 3](#3-sign-in-to-emploist).

### Everything Claude can do

- **Overview:** your role, plan, and AI credits; hiring statistics for the last 7, 30, or 90 days; your team; your notifications.
- **Jobs:** find jobs; read a job's details, application form, and stages; create a job as a draft or publish it; edit its details or application form; publish, open, close, archive, or restore it; duplicate or delete it.
- **Stages:** add, rename, reorder, or delete a job's stages; switch a stage's email to applicants on or off.
- **Applications:** list a stage's applications with their AI matches; read an application with its answers, comments, and resume; move it to another stage; undo your latest move; rank it again if ranking failed; delete it.
- **Comments:** comment on an application and mention teammates; edit or delete your own comments (admins can delete anyone's).
- **Company settings, for admins:** turn AI ranking on or off; rank or skip applications that came in while you had no AI credits; turn emails to applicants on or off; choose where applicants' replies go.

Some things stay in Emploist: billing, members and invitations, deleting your company or account, your careers page's code and domain, email layouts, and the text of stage emails.

## Stay in control

**Claude works as you.** It has the same access you have in Emploist, and nothing more. If you're a member, Claude can't change company settings either.

**You choose what Claude can do without asking.** In Claude, open **Customize** → **Connectors** → **Emploist** to set permissions for its read-only tools and for those that make changes: **Always allow**, **Needs approval**, or **Blocked**.

These permissions apply in **Manual** mode, which you choose next to the model, below the message box. In **Auto** mode, Claude can act without asking, and pauses to ask only if something looks unsafe.

**Emploist warns Claude about applicants' text.** It tells Claude that names, answers, and resumes are written by applicants, and never to follow instructions in them. If an application contains text that looks like instructions for an AI assistant, Emploist flags it, and Claude tells you.

**You can disconnect anytime.** On Emploist's AI assistants page, click **Disconnect** next to Claude, and it stops working for you right away. You can connect it again whenever you like.

**Admins can turn it off for everyone.** In **Settings** → **General**, the **MCP** switch decides whether your team can use AI assistants with Emploist at all. Turning it off stops every connected assistant until you turn it back on.

## Good to know

- **It's included in your plan.** Ranking an application from Claude uses one AI credit, just like in Emploist. Nothing else Claude does uses credits.
- **Each person connects their own Claude**, and it works with their own role.
- **Check Claude's work.** Claude can make mistakes. Read its answers, especially before moving applicants or publishing a job.
- **Other assistants work too.** The AI assistants page has steps for ChatGPT, Claude Code, Cursor, VS Code, and any other assistant that supports MCP.
